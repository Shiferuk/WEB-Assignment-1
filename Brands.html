Chicago Bulls

Founded: 1966 | City: Chicago, Illinois

The Chicago Bulls became a global sports icon during the 1990s, winning six NBA championships behind legendary players like Michael Jordan and Scottie Pippen. Based in Chicago, Illinois, the Bulls are recognized worldwide for their fierce red and black identity, representing resilience, championship culture, and elite basketball history.

Los Angeles Lakers

Founded: 1947 | City: Los Angeles, California

Originally founded in Minneapolis before moving to Los Angeles in 1960, the Lakers are one of the most successful franchises in NBA history with 17 championship titles. Known for their famous "Showtime" era and superstars like Magic Johnson, Kobe Bryant, and LeBron James, the Lakers embody Hollywood glamour combined with relentless athletic excellence.

Golden State Warriors

Founded: 1946 | City: San Francisco, California

Founded in Philadelphia and later relocating to the San Francisco Bay Area, the Warriors revolutionized modern basketball with their high-speed, three-point shooting style. Led by Stephen Curry, the team established a modern dynasty, winning four championships in less than a decade and inspiring millions of young players around the world.
